## Remaining source ambiguity and quality controls

- Numbered color cards (especially Paris Japan, Zoya, Gana, Highlight, Airtech, mukena collections) require reading before assigning names. `unclassifiedAssets` retains uncertain files instead of turning random UUIDs into colors. Generic consultation variants honestly indicate this; they are not evidence of full per-color verification.
- Source filenames contain anomalies: a Paris Jadul mustard image is inside Hawa, a pashmina oval size guide inside Aisha, PPV-D3 contains multiple PPV-B1 filenames. Prefer source-folder + visual confirmation rather than blindly parsing filenames.
- Content Reseller videos mention historical products such as Baby Paris, Julia Instan, Amira Instant, Pashmina Cotton Viscose and Rayon Airflow. These lack a current dedicated product folder and September price row. Retain source videos in the manifest; do not infer current sellable products or fabricated prices from old promotional names.
- The PDF contains no verified telephone number. Do not reuse the reference demo WhatsApp contact. Official Instagram account and linktree contact verification is being handled separately by the parent task.
- Do not claim every color is current stock or that the entire Drive has been downloaded. The hierarchy and metadata have been fully enumerated; the website uses curated official assets with provenance.
